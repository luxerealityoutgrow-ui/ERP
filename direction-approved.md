# Design Direction Gate - CRM Forms Visual Adjustment

This document records the visual direction exploration and selection under the `/huashu-design` workflow for adjusting the creation and edit forms (Lead, Property, Deal) in the Luxe Realty ERP CRM tool.

## Prototyped Visual Directions

Three distinct high-fidelity form layout and input style directions have been designed and rendered in `design-demos/form-options.html`.

### Direction 01: Linear-Sleek Modernism (Quiet Premium)
* **Philosophy**: Clean, hyper-focused, minimal SaaS interface (reminiscent of Linear or Vercel). High-density grid structure, borderless inputs, dark slate accents.
* **Mock-up Screen**: [design-demos/form-options.html](file:///Users/anw3r/AI%20Projects/Luxe%20Realty%20Pune%20-%20ERP/design-demos/form-options.html) (Panel 1)

### Direction 02: Editorial Gold Signature (Bespoke / Custom Brand Style)
* **Philosophy**: Warm, high-end editorial look suited for luxury real estate. Spaced labels, rounded warm porcelain grey input wells, serif numeral markers, and gold halo focus rings.
* **Mock-up Screen**: [design-demos/form-options.html](file:///Users/anw3r/AI%20Projects/Luxe%20Realty%20Pune%20-%20ERP/design-demos/form-options.html) (Panel 2)

### Direction 03: Neo-Glassmorphic Studio (Tactile Depth)
* **Philosophy**: A modern, interactive, tactile feel with layered inset shading, pill-shaped selects, drop-shadow offset fields, and pulsing indicators.
* **Mock-up Screen**: [design-demos/form-options.html](file:///Users/anw3r/AI%20Projects/Luxe%20Realty%20Pune%20-%20ERP/design-demos/form-options.html) (Panel 3)

## User Choice Record
* **Selected Direction**: Option 02 (Editorial Gold Signature) for mobile / smaller viewports.
* **User Feedback / Notes**: Apply the warm editorial style (Gold tracked labels, soft rounded warm inputs, halo glow focus states, serif numerals) specifically to mobile/smaller viewports.
